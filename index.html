A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

Let the rules do the making.

  1. Begin with a shape. Scatter creates a seeded point cloud. The same seed gives the same beginning.
  2. Shape the flow. Click an operator to edit it. Bypass, move, or remove steps. Order matters.
  3. Find your frame. Play or scrub the eight-second study. Drag the canvas to orbit.
  4. Keep a copy. Save an editable .fluxgrove project or export the current view as a 1600 × 1000 PNG.

Space plays or pauses outside controls. Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes edits. Focus the canvas for arrow-key orbit, +/− zoom, and Home to reset.

An original procedural point-art kata inspired by Houdini. Not a physics simulator, a mesh modeler, or a Houdini-compatible editor. The animation loops by restarting at frame zero; it is not guaranteed seamless.
